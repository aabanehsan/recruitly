What gets cleaned up

  • Columns are matched to profile fields from the question text ("What's your major?" → Major). You can fix any mapping in the preview.
  • Names get proper capitalization (jOHN mcdonald → John McDonald). A single "Full name" question is split into first and last.
  • Emails are lowercased, common typos are fixed (@utexas.com → @utexas.edu), and invalid ones are flagged.
  • Majors like "cs", "comp sci" or "MIS" are matched to the official major list, and "CS and Math" becomes two majors.
  • Phones, GPAs, graduation terms, LinkedIn and Instagram are put into one consistent format.
  • Duplicates: if someone submits twice, only their newest response is kept. People already in the system are updated instead of duplicated.
  • Headshots from file-upload questions are downloaded, cropped to a square and resized. If Drive blocks the download, use the bulk headshot upload.
